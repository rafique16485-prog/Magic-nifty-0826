Institutional Discipline & Mindset Bible

Option buying mein 90% traders strategy ki wajah se nahi, emotion aur indiscipline ki wajah se loss karte hain.

10 Golden Rules of Option Buying

1. Strictly ATM / Slight ITM Only Saste OTM (Out-of-the-Money) premium lottery ticket hote hain. Hamesha 0.50 Delta wala ATM strike chunein.
2. Max 2 Trades Per Day Overtrading option buyers ka sabse bada dushman hai. 2 trades ke baad market chahe jahan jaye, screen band karein.
3. Fixed 10-Point Hard Stop Loss Dimag mein Stop Loss mat rakhein; terminal par system SL lagayein. 10 point se zyada ka risk option buyer ke liye zehar hai.
4. Respect 1:2 R:R Rule (70-30 Formula) 20 points (+1:2) aate hi 70% quantity lock karein aur baaki 30% ka SL cost-to-cost trail karein.
5. The Lunch Chop Trap (11:30 - 01:15) Is waqt institutions lunch par hote hain aur Theta decay premiums ko kha jata hai. Fresh entry bilkul avoid karein.
6. Revenge Trading Kill-Switch Agar subah ka trade Stop Loss hit kar de, toh gusse mein double quantity ke saath market se badla na lein.
